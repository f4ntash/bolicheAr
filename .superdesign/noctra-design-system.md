# NOCTRA — Interactive Festival Experience

## Product context

NOCTRA is a mobile-first, premium electronic festival experience for fictional Mendoza 2026. Its attendee flow includes entrance, main stage, a hidden frequency, the fictional NOVA sponsor activation, a digital festival pass, photo frames, sharing and a final reward. This is a conceptual demo; no real event, artist, sponsor, or prize is represented.

## Art direction

Nocturnal editorial energy: contemporary festival photography, monumental architectural forms, soft haze, deep black, smoke, warm off-white, and controlled coral light. Use restrained violet or blue only as a secondary reflection. Composition should feel tactile, cinematic, clean, and expensive. Large typography, high contrast, thin rules, asymmetry, layered depth, and purposeful negative space. Avoid cyberpunk, generic neon, gamer UI, crypto glass, tourism, SaaS layouts, real event references, and real artist or company names.

- Coral light: `#e66b50`
- Restrained violet light: `#706184`
- Hairline: `rgba(243, 238, 231, .16)`

## Typography

- Display: `Cormorant Garamond`, fallback `Georgia`, used selectively for emotional statements.
- Interface: `DM Sans`, fallback `Arial`, for controls, navigation, labels, and body copy.
- Short uppercase labels use letter spacing. Body text stays conversational and brief.

## Layout and behavior

- Mobile is the source of truth at 390×844 and 430×932; respect safe areas and dynamic viewport height.
- A full-bleed hero uses local abstract festival artwork with slow moving light beams and restrained particles. Keep text and controls legible over it.
- Desktop expands into a broad editorial layout; it does not retain a phone frame.
- Station art must have distinct compositions within one consistent visual family.
- Passport resembles a backstage credential or digital wristband, with clear station states and reward progress.
- Motion supports the atmosphere and unlock feedback. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Use responsive controls with visible focus and no horizontal overflow.

## Asset invariant

Use the supplied NOCTRA Brand Asset logo in every logo position. Do not substitute initials, emoji, generic symbols, invented marks, or text alone. All event, artist, sponsor, and reward content is fictional.
